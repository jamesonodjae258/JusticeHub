JusticeHubPhase 2 — Product Requirements Document  v2.0Category: Legal Technology / Practice ManagementVersion: 2.0 — Updated DraftDate: July 2026Builds on: JusticeHub MVP PRD v0.1Phase 2 v2.0 adds: Role hierarchy, Super Admin, Firm Admin, Profiles & Settings1. Context & RationalePhase 1 established JusticeHub's core loop: shared case management, document hub, client portal, court dates, and internal collaboration. Phase 2 converts that foundation into a revenue-replacing workflow tool — one where a firm can run their entire practice from intake to invoice inside a single product.This v2.0 update adds four new scope areas to Phase 2 that were missing from v1.0:Role hierarchy — a formal permission system with Super Admin, Firm Admin, Attorney, Staff, and Client rolesUser management — the ability for a Firm Admin to invite and manage attorneys and staffProfiles — every user has an editable profile with avatar, contact info, and role-specific settingsSettings — firm-level and personal-level settings surfaces, plus a Super Admin control panelPrerequisite:  Complete the pilot debrief checklist with at least 3 pilot firms before beginning Phase 2 build. Do not build on an unvalidated foundation.2. Role Hierarchy & Permission SystemJusticeHub has two tiers of roles: platform-level roles (managed by JusticeHub) and firm-level roles (managed within each firm). These tiers never overlap — a Super Admin cannot access firm-level billing or cases; a Firm Admin cannot access the platform control panel.2.1 Role DefinitionsSuper Admin  (Platform level — JusticeHub team only)The Super Admin is a JusticeHub-internal role, not available to law firm users. There is typically one Super Admin account with the option to grant secondary Super Admin access to trusted team members.Can view all firm accounts, user counts, case counts, and subscription status on a platform dashboardCan view any client document across any firm for support and audit purposes — read-only, never editable or downloadableCan suspend or reinstate any firm accountCan manage platform-wide settings: feature flags, plan configurations, email template overrides, maintenance modeCan view platform-level analytics: total firms, active cases, invoices generated, documents signed, monthly active usersCannot alter, delete, or download client documentsCannot impersonate a firm user or act on behalf of a firmCannot view billing details, invoice amounts, or time entry values of firmsSecurity note:  Super Admin access must be protected by two-factor authentication (2FA) enforced at login. All Super Admin actions are logged to a separate immutable audit table with IP address, timestamp, and action description.Firm Admin  (Firm level — assigned within each firm)The Firm Admin is a designated member of a law firm responsible for managing the firm's JusticeHub workspace. This is typically a practice manager, senior partner, or office administrator. Each firm has at least one Firm Admin and can have multiple.Can invite attorneys (lawyers) and staff to the firm workspace via emailCan set roles when inviting: Attorney or StaffCan deactivate a firm member's access (does not delete their work — only revokes login)Can manage firm-level settings: firm name, logo, address, default hourly rate, payment terms, invoice templateCan view all cases within the firm and assign cases to attorneysCan generate and send invoices on behalf of any attorney in the firmCannot view the contents of client documents — can see document names and upload dates, but not the document contentCannot access the JusticeHub Super Admin panelDesign principle:  The Firm Admin's inability to view document contents is a deliberate legal ethics boundary. In many jurisdictions, non-lawyer staff accessing privileged client documents creates professional responsibility issues. This constraint must be enforced at the RLS and storage URL level — not just the UI.Attorney  (Firm level — the primary working user)Can create, view, and update cases assigned to themCan view all cases in the firm (visibility), but can only edit cases they are assigned to (write access)Can upload, view, and tag all documents on cases they are assigned toCan log time entries and use the timer on their assigned casesCan generate invoices for their cases (Firm Admin can also do this on their behalf)Can send e-signature requests to clientsCan invite clients to the client portal from a caseCan add internal notes and view the full case activity logCannot access firm settings, billing configuration, or other attorneys' time entriesStaff / Paralegal  (Firm level — support role)Can view all cases in the firmCan upload documents to cases and tag them — cannot view document contents unless explicitly granted on a per-case basis by an AttorneyCan add court dates and case eventsCan add internal notesCannot log billable time entries or generate invoicesCannot invite clients or send e-signature requestsCannot access firm settingsClient  (Firm level — read-only portal access)Can log into the client portal using the invite link sent by an attorneyCan view their case status, upcoming dates, and documents explicitly shared with themCannot edit, download (unless attorney enables it), or comment on anythingCannot see other clients or other casesCannot access the main JusticeHub app — only the client portal surface2.2 Permissions User Management ModuleUser management is the operational surface through which a Firm Admin builds and maintains their team inside JusticeHub. It is the Firm Admin's primary responsibility beyond case oversight.3.1 Inviting team membersFirm Admin opens the Team section in firm settingsClicks 'Invite member' — enters name, email address, and selects role (Attorney or Staff)JusticeHub sends an invitation email with a secure signup link (24-hour expiry)Invitee clicks the link, sets their password, and their account is created with the assigned roleFirm Admin sees the invite status (Pending / Accepted) in the team listIf an invite expires, the Firm Admin can resend it with one click3.2 Managing existing membersFirm Admin can view all team members: name, role, email, join date, and last active dateCan change a member's role between Attorney and Staff (not to Firm Admin — that requires a separate, deliberate promotion action)Can deactivate a member — this immediately revokes their login but preserves all their case notes, time entries, and documentsCan promote another member to Firm Admin — requires the current Firm Admin to confirm with their passwordDeactivated members appear in a separate 'Deactivated' list and can be reactivated3.3 Data model additions for user managementField / TableNotesfirm_invitations tableid, firm_id, email, role, invited_by, token (UUID), expires_at, accepted_at — separate from users table until acceptedusers.roleExtended to: super_admin | firm_admin | attorney | staff | client — enforced at RLS levelusers.statusactive | deactivated — deactivated blocks login at auth layer, does not delete the rowusers.deactivated_atTimestamp of deactivation for audit purposesusers.deactivated_byuser_id of the Firm Admin who performed the deactivationsuper_admin_audit_logid, super_admin_id, action, target_type, target_id, ip_address, created_at — immutable log of all Super Admin actions4. Profiles ModuleEvery JusticeHub user — attorney, staff, firm admin, and client — has a profile. Profiles serve two purposes: personalisation (avatar, display name) and professional credibility (bar number, specialisation, contact details shown to clients via the portal).4.1 Attorney / Staff / Firm Admin profileEditable by the user themselvesProfile photo (avatar) — uploaded image, displayed in the sidebar, case cards, and client portalFull name — displayed as it will appear to clientsProfessional title — e.g. 'Senior Associate', 'Partner', 'Paralegal'Email address — used for login and notifications (change requires email verification)Phone number — optional; shown to clients if the attorney enables itBio — short professional summary, shown to clients in the portal (140 character limit)Attorney-only fieldsBar / roll number — optional professional credential displayed on client-facing surfacesPractice areas — multi-select: Civil, Criminal, Corporate, Family, Property, Immigration, Labour, OtherHourly rate — pre-fills when logging time on cases; overridable per caseNot editable by the userRole — only a Firm Admin can change thisFirm — cannot be changed from the profile; requires a new invite to a different firmJoin date — system-generated4.2 Client profileClients have a minimal profile — just enough to be identified in the portal and contacted by the firm.Full nameEmail address (used for portal login and invoice delivery)Phone number (optional)Preferred language (for future localisation support)Client profiles are created by the inviting attorney, not by the client themselves at signup. The client can edit name, phone, and language preference after accepting the portal invite.4.3 Profile data modelFieldNotesuser_idPrimary key — 1:1 with auth userfirm_idFirm the user belongs to — enforces RLSdisplay_nameFull name as shown to clients and in the apptitleProfessional title — free text, max 60 charsavatar_urlSupabase Storage URL — signed URL with 7-day TTL; regenerated on loadbioShort bio — max 140 charsphoneOptional — stored encryptedbar_numberAttorney only — optionalpractice_areasAttorney only — text arrayhourly_rateAttorney only — numeric, stored in firm currencyshow_phone_to_clientsBoolean — attorney controls whether phone is visible in portalnotification_preferencesJSONB — stores per-channel notification settings (see Section 6)5. Settings ModuleSettings is split into three surfaces depending on the user's role: Personal Settings (all users), Firm Settings (Firm Admin only), and Platform Settings (Super Admin only). Each surface is accessible from a 'Settings' entry in the sidebar.5.1 Personal Settings  (all users)AccountChange display nameChange email — requires current password confirmation and email verification to new addressChange password — requires current password, new password, and confirmationEnable / disable two-factor authentication (2FA) — TOTP-based (Google Authenticator / Authy)Delete account — requires password confirmation and shows a warning that this action cannot be undone; Firm Admin must first reassign or close all active casesProfileAll profile fields from Section 4.1 / 4.2 — edit and save from this surfaceUpload or remove profile photoNotificationsEmail notifications: toggle on/off per event typeIn-app notifications: toggle on/off per event typeNotification event types:Case assigned to meNew document uploaded to my caseClient viewed the portalDocument signed by clientInvoice marked as paidCourt date in 48 hoursNew internal note added to my caseInvoice overdue reminder (Firm Admin only)AppearanceLight mode only in Phase 2 — this toggle is present but shows 'Dark mode coming soon'Language — English (default); additional languages in Phase 35.2 Firm Settings  (Firm Admin only)Firm profileFirm name — displayed on invoices, client portal header, and email signaturesFirm logo — uploaded image; displayed on invoices and client portalFirm address — displayed on invoicesPrimary contact email — the 'reply-to' address on all firm-sent emailsPhone number — shown on invoices and client portalWebsite URL — optional; shown on client portalBilling defaultsDefault hourly rate — pre-fills for all new attorneys; each attorney can override in their profileDefault payment terms — e.g. 'Payment due within 14 days' — appears on all invoicesInvoice currency — set once at firm level; all invoices use this currencyInvoice number format — e.g. INV-2026-001 — configurable prefix and auto-incrementTax label and default rate — e.g. 'VAT 7.5%' — appears as an optional line on invoicesBank account details — shown on invoices as payment instructions (manual payment in Phase 2)Team managementView all team members with role, status, and last active dateInvite new attorney or staff member (flow from Section 3.1)Change member roleDeactivate / reactivate memberPromote to Firm AdminView pending invites and resend expired onesClient portal settingsPortal header message — a short welcome message shown to clients when they log in (max 200 chars)Enable / disable client ability to download shared documentsShow / hide attorney phone number to clients by default (attorneys can override per profile)SecurityEnforce 2FA for all firm members — when enabled, all members must set up 2FA within 7 days or lose accessView login activity for all firm members: date, time, IP address, deviceSession timeout — configurable: 1 hour, 4 hours, 8 hours, 24 hours, 7 days5.3 Platform Settings  (Super Admin only)Firm managementView all registered firms: name, plan, member count, case count, created date, last activeSearch firms by name or emailView a firm's basic stats without accessing case content or documentsSuspend a firm account — immediately locks all firm members out; firm data is preservedReinstate a suspended firmDelete a firm — requires typed confirmation of firm name; irreversible; sends data export to Firm Admin email firstPlatform analyticsTotal registered firms, broken down by plan tierMonthly active users (MAU) across all firmsTotal cases created, active cases, closed casesTotal documents uploadedTotal invoices generated and total amount (aggregate — no firm-specific breakdown visible to Super Admin)Total e-signature requests sent and completion rateNew firm registrations over time (chart)Feature flagsToggle Phase 2 modules on/off globally or per firm — e.g. enable billing for pilot firms onlyEnable/disable e-signature integrationToggle maintenance mode — displays a maintenance banner to all users; Super Admin can still access the panelDocument audit accessSuper Admin can search for a specific document by case ID or document name across all firms for support purposesDocuments are displayed in a read-only viewer — no download button, no edit capabilityEvery document view by a Super Admin is logged to the super_admin_audit_log with timestamp, document ID, and actionEmail & notification templatesView and override platform-level email templates: invite email, portal invite, invoice delivery, overdue reminder, signing requestTemplates use a simple variable system: {{firm_name}}, {{client_name}}, {{case_name}}, {{link}}Changes to templates apply globally unless a firm has a custom override (firm override wins)6. Module A — Time TrackingAttorneys currently track billable hours in a separate spreadsheet or not at all. Time is reconstructed from memory at invoice time — inaccurate, underbilled, and time-consuming. JusticeHub already has the case and the attorney; time tracking closes the loop.6.1 In scopeTime entry on a case: attorney logs hours, date, rate, and a description of work performedTimer: a running clock inside the case view that an attorney can start/stop and convert to a time entry on completionPer-case time log: chronological view of all time entries with running total hours and total billable amountBillable vs non-billable toggle per entryHourly rate per attorney set in their profile; overridable per case or per entryTime entries feed directly into the billing module — no re-entry required6.2 Time entry data modelFieldNotescase_idLinks the entry to a specific caseuser_idThe attorney who logged the timefirm_idRLS scopedateDate the work was performed — not necessarily todayduration_minutesStored in minutes; displayed as h:mmrate_per_hourPulled from attorney profile default; overridablebillable_amountComputed: (duration_minutes / 60) x rate_per_houris_billableBoolean — defaults to truedescriptionFree text — becomes the invoice line iteminvoice_idNull until billed — prevents double-billing6.3 Out of scopeAutomated time capture — Phase 3Client-visible time log — clients see the invoice, not the time entriesPayroll or contractor payments — out of scope entirely7. Module B — Billing & InvoicingJusticeHub should own the entire billing cycle: generate, send, track, and mark paid. The invoice is generated from time entries already in the system — attorneys review and approve, not re-type.7.1 In scopeGenerate invoice from a case: pulls all unbilled time entries; option to add flat-fee line items manuallyInvoice preview: attorney reviews line items, adjusts descriptions, sets due date and payment termsSend invoice to client via email — PDF attachment + link to a hosted invoice view (no login required)Invoice status: Draft → Sent → Viewed → Paid → OverdueMark invoice as paid (manual in Phase 2 — payment gateway is Phase 3)Invoice history per case and per client, filterable by statusOverdue reminders: automated email at 7 days and 14 days after due date if unpaidInvoice PDF uses firm name, logo, address, and bank details from Firm Settings (Section 5.2)7.2 Invoice data modelFieldNotesinvoice_idPrimary keycase_idThe case this invoice belongs toclient_idDenormalised from case for faster queriesfirm_idRLS scopestatusdraft | sent | viewed | paid | overdueissue_dateDate invoice was generateddue_dateSet by the attorney at creationline_itemsJSONB array: [{description, quantity, unit_price, amount}]subtotalSum of all line item amountstax_amountManual entry — firm adds if applicabletotal_amountsubtotal + tax_amountpdf_urlSupabase Storage URL to the generated invoice PDFsent_atTimestamp of invoice email sendpaid_atTimestamp of manual mark-paid actionreminder_sent_atTracks last reminder to prevent duplicates7.3 Out of scopeOnline payment processing (Paystack, Stripe, Flutterwave) — Phase 3Recurring invoices / retainer billing — Phase 3Expense tracking and reimbursables — Phase 3Accounting software integrations (QuickBooks, Xero) — Phase 38. Module C — Document E-signatureJusticeHub already holds the document. E-signature closes the loop by letting clients sign from the client portal without touching paper. The feature is built on a third-party provider — not from scratch.8.1 Provider selectionDocusign API — recommended; most legally recognised globally; solid Nigeria supportAdobe Sign — strong legal recognition; good APIDocuSeal — open-source; self-hostable; lower costDecision required:  Choose the e-signature provider before this module is built. Confirm legal standing of electronic signatures for your target jurisdiction before launch.8.2 In scopeAttorney flags any document in the case document hub as 'requires signature'Attorney sends a signature request to the client from the case detail viewClient receives an email with a signing link — no account creation requiredClient reviews the document and signs using a drawn or typed signatureSigned document is stored in the case document hub with status 'Signed'Both attorney and client receive a copy of the signed document by emailAudit trail provided by the third-party API: signer identity, IP address, timestamp8.3 E-signature data modelFieldNotesidPrimary keycase_idThe case this request belongs todocument_idThe specific document requiring signaturefirm_idRLS scopeclient_idThe client being asked to signproviderdocusign | adobesign | docusealprovider_envelope_idThird-party reference for audit trail retrievalstatuspending | signed | declined | expiredrequested_atTimestamp of signature request sendsigned_atTimestamp of completed signaturesigned_doc_urlSupabase Storage URL to the signed document8.4 Out of scopeMulti-party signing (e.g. two attorneys + client) — Phase 3In-app document editor or template builder — Phase 3Biometric or government-ID-backed signing — Phase 39. Risks & MitigationsRiskDetailMitigationDocument access boundary (Firm Admin)Firm Admin seeing document contents even read-only could create legal ethics violationsEnforce the restriction at Supabase Storage signed URL level — Firm Admin role never receives a signed URL for document content, only for document metadataSuper Admin document audit misuseSuper Admin read access to all documents is a powerful and sensitive capabilityImmutable audit log on every document view. Access is logged with IP and timestamp. Periodic audit review.Role escalationA Staff member manipulating their JWT claim to gain Attorney-level accessRole is verified server-side on every request against the database, not just the JWT claim. JWT claims are signed and not user-modifiable — but always double-check at the DB layer.Invitation token reuseA leaked invitation link being used by someone other than the intended recipientTokens are one-time-use and expire in 24 hours. Acceptance marks the token as used immediately.PDF generation at scaleComplex invoices slow to generate as firm usage growsWarm Edge Functions on firm login.
